Understand the Problem

Root causes, operational context, and why manual verification fails at border checkposts

🚨

The Real-World Context

Border checkpoints process thousands of documents every day under intense time pressure (typically under 45 seconds per passenger).

  • Indo-Nepal (1,751 km) & Indo-Bhutan (699 km): Open, regulated border regimes where honest citizens cross without visas.
  • Severe Bottlenecks: Peak commuter hours cause traffic jams at ICPs (Raxaul, Sonauli).
  • Human Limit: Fatigue leads to missed forgeries after 8-hour shifts.
🔍

Root Causes of Vulnerability

Criminal syndicates and illegal infiltrators no longer use crude paper fakes; they use digital tools:

  • Photo Splicing: Cutting genuine card lamination, replacing the photo, and re-laminating.
  • Photoshop VIZ Edits: Altering printed birth dates to look younger or evade watchlists.
  • Dual-Zone Discrepancy: Changing the visual text but leaving the machine-readable MRZ untouched.
  • Third-Country Masquerading: Foreigners forging Indian Voter IDs to sneak past open borders without a visa.
🧩

What Makes It Hard to Solve

Existing tools fail because they assume a clean, air-conditioned airport environment:

  • Telecom Shadow Zones: Frequent cable cuts and zero broadband at remote checkposts.
  • Multi-Document Variety: Border travelers carry Voter IDs, Driving Licenses, and Nepalese Nagarikta—not just passports.
  • Screen Replay Spoofing: Impersonators holding smartphone screens of real owners.

Technical Decisions: Offline Edge Architecture & Hardware Deployment Matrix

Rigorous engineering justifications answering (1) How we run 100% offline at the edge and what minimal operations require connectivity, and (2) Exact operational criteria governing when to deploy fixed PCs, rugged mobile tablets, and eye-level webcams across India's 2,450 km open borders.

DECISION PILLAR 1

1. How We Run Offline & What Parts Require Network (With Tech Used)

Detailed technical decomposition of our air-gapped inference engine vs. targeted asynchronous synchronization.

📡 The Operational Imperative: Why Offline-First is Non-Negotiable
75% Fiber Deficit (DoT 2025)

Rajya Sabha Q498 records prove ~75% of border Gram Panchayats in Uttarakhand lack broadband readiness; national rural uptime target (95%) is achieved at only 48%.

14-Day Landslide Blackouts

DoT Year-End 2025 records show monsoon landslides in border districts (Pithoragarh, Chamoli, J&K) regularly cut optical fiber cables for up to 14 continuous days.

Satellite Low Bandwidth

Border Outposts (BOPs) rely on POLNET/VSAT satellite links offering only 32–64 kbps—wholly unable to stream live camera feeds or heavy cloud API payloads.

⚡
100% Offline Edge Pipeline
Zero Internet Used

All screening decisions, document forensics, and biometric verifications execute locally on the device in < 2.8 seconds without making a single external web call.

1. Image Preprocessing & Glare Suppression OpenCV (C++)

Tech: CLAHE (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization) + 4-point contour perspective warp. Runs locally in ~35ms to neutralize plastic lamination glare and align tilted cards.

2. Multilingual OCR & Script Extraction PaddleOCR INT8

Tech: ONNX Runtime with INT8 quantization (~18 MB weights). Parses English OCR-B, Hindi (Devanagari), Nepali (Devanagari), and Dzongkha locally in ~320ms on standard CPU without cloud APIs.

3. ICAO Doc 9303 Checksum Math & Logic Pure Math Engine

Tech: Deterministic modulo-10 validator using official [7, 3, 1, 7, 3, 1...] weighting vector. Cross-checks VIZ vs. MRZ parity and date chronologies in < 5ms.

4. Pixel-Level Forensics & ELA AI OpenCV / NumPy

Tech: Error Level Analysis (ELA) recompressing at 90% quality + Laplacian edge variance filter. Spotlights spliced photos and razor-blade cut borders as glowing crimson heatmaps in ~180ms.

5. 1:1 Facial Biometrics & Anti-Spoofing ArcFace / YuNet

Tech: 512-dimensional vector embedding comparison (Cosine similarity > 0.82) + Fourier frequency anti-spoofing blocking phone screens and photo print replays in ~80ms.

6. 10-Lakh Commuter Cache & Watchlist SQLite + FAISS

Tech: 10 GB edge storage holds 1,000,000 resident 512-d embeddings. FAISS HNSW index searches 10 Lakh records in < 15ms locally without server queries.

7. Cryptographic SHA-256 Audit Trail Hash Chain Ledger

Tech: Append-only cryptographic hash chain linking previous block hash, officer ID, GPS, and document digest. Court-admissible under BSA with zero network needed.

🌐
Targeted Asynchronous Network Sync
Only 5% Exceptions

Network is NEVER used in the critical sub-3s primary clearance path. Connectivity is strictly utilized for background queue sync and deep Tier-2 anomaly resolution.

1. Tier-2 Deep Watchlist Query (Targeted Anomalies) mTLS REST / gRPC

When Online: Only when an individual is flagged as high risk (Score > 30) or is an unverified third-country national. The system queries Central IVFRT, Interpol Red Notices, and CCTNS Police FIRs. Saves 95% of satellite bandwidth!

2. Delta Watchlist Updates (Merkle Differential Sync) Merkle Trees / HTTP2

Tech Used: Cryptographic Merkle-tree diffs. When the device detects internet (cellular or satellite), it only downloads newly cancelled passports or newly added wanted vectors (50 KB to 2 MB delta payload), never the entire 10 GB master database.

3. Cryptographic Audit Uplink (Store & Forward) MQTT / SQLite WAL

Tech Used: Lightweight MQTT broker with QoS level 2 (exactly once delivery) and SQLite Write-Ahead Logging. Completed audit blocks are queued locally. When connectivity restores, batches stream to MHA central servers with automatic pause/resume.

4. Over-The-Air Model & Treaty Policy Updates Docker OCI Registry

Tech Used: Cryptographically signed container layers. When bilateral treaties change (e.g. new district DAO format), policy JSON rules or quantized ONNX models sync securely without recompiling edge client software.

Bandwidth Optimization Summary:

Primary screening uses 0 KB/s. Background delta sync uses < 5 KB per hour under normal conditions, operating reliably even over unstable 2G or 32 kbps POLNET links.
